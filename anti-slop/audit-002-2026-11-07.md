# antislop audit 002: links and claims outside /projects

Date: 2026-11-07. Mode: after. Scope: Home, About, Services, Blog index, footer, page metadata, and the external links on those pages.

Method: every `href` on `/`, `/about`, `/services`, `/projects`, `/blog` in production was requested (104 links), and the failures were opened in a browser. Copy was read from `components/sections/*`, `app/(public)/*/page.tsx`, and `lib/site-metadata.ts`, and each number or claim was checked against `lib/data/*` and the case studies.
